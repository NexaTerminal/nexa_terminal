# Nexa — Explainer / Feature Tour Video (60–90s)

> Audience: **SMB owners, HR, office managers (Basic / nexa.mk)**. Not lawyers. Don't mix in Pro messaging.
> Goal: "This is where I get informed, do less admin, and reach a professional when I need one."
> Language: Macedonian, formal **Вие**. Style: Nexa web app (Inter, blue #1E4DB7, teal accent, white cards).
> Sources: `nexaProduct.md`, `NEXA_3.0_TESTING_GUIDE.md` (⚠ the testing guide describes the older 3-tier/trial
> model; the video follows the **current** model: Basic is free and self-serve, there is no trial, AI Тим has 5 characters, and "Покани професионалец" (pro joins the AI chat) is live).

---

## 1. Format & deliverables

| Version | Ratio | Length | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Master** | 9:16 (1080×1920) | **75s** | TikTok, Instagram Reels, FB Reels, YouTube Shorts |
| Cutdown A | 9:16 | 30s | Paid ads, Stories (Hook → AI → Document → CTA) |
| Cutdown B | 4:5 (1080×1350) | 60s | LinkedIn feed (native upload), FB/IG feed |
| Cutdown C | 9:16 | 15s | Stories / retargeting (one problem → one screen → CTA) |

- **Captions burned in**, on every frame. Most feed video plays muted, so the on-screen text has to carry the whole story without sound.
- Voice-over is optional. AI voices for Macedonian are weak; if you add VO, record it yourself (phone + quiet room is enough).
- Music: upbeat electronic / corporate-pop, **120–128 BPM**. The drop lands on the logo reveal (≈0:08) and every cut follows the beat.
  On TikTok/Instagram business accounts, use only the **Commercial Music Library** or royalty-free tracks (Artlist, Epidemic, Pixabay).

## 2. Visual style (from the app's design system)

| Token | Value | Use in video |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | `#1E4DB7` | Headlines, buttons, highlight rings |
| Deep navy | `#0F2766` / `#13317A` | Background for title cards |
| Light blue | `#7BB8FF` / `#E0EEFF` | Aurora orbs, soft panels |
| Teal accent | `#2BB3C0` | Second aurora orb, "✓" ticks |
| Orange | `#F97316` | ONLY for the problem/risk moments (warning, fine) |
| Neutrals | `#FFFFFF`, `#F5F5F5`, `#262626` | Cards, text |
| Font | **Inter**: 800 headlines, 600 labels, 500 body | All text |

- Look: aurora gradient backgrounds (blurred blue + teal orbs drifting slowly), white rounded cards (12–16px radius,
  1px light border, soft shadow), clean and monochrome, no clip-art and no stock "handshake" photos.
- Screen recordings sit in a floating browser frame (or phone frame) with a slight 3D tilt and drop shadow.
  Use **zoom-to-cursor** punch-ins, a blue highlight ring on whatever gets clicked, and speed ramps (record slowly, play back at 1.5–3×).
- Transitions: fast whip/slide on the beat, cards popping in with a slight overshoot, word-by-word kinetic type for the hook.
- Text on screen: maximum **6–7 words per card**, big, centered in the safe zone (keep the top 15% and bottom 20% clear of TikTok/Reels UI).

## 5. What to record (screen-video shot list)

### Recording setup (do this once)
- **Demo account only**, with a fake company e.g. „Пример Трејд ДООЕЛ", Скопје, and fake employees. No real user, client or lead data on screen.
- Chrome, **1920×1080, browser zoom 125%**, bookmarks bar hidden, extensions hidden, incognito-clean profile, notifications off.
  Also record **R14 in mobile viewport** (DevTools → iPhone 14, 390×844) for native-vertical shots.
- Tool: Screen Studio / Cleanshot / OBS at 60fps. Turn **cursor highlight on**, cursor size large.
- Move **slowly and deliberately**, pause 1s before and after every click (the editor speeds it up). Record each clip **2×**.
- Pre-type long questions in a note and paste them, or type slowly. Keep each clip 8–25s.
- **Do not show**: admin menu, Виртуелен саем, Барање за понуди / Sourcing, prices/billing, "Сите барања", any lead data.
